\# VANVIDHYA 🌱



\### Unified Scholarship \& Student Support Platform for Tribal Students



VANVIDHYA is a unified digital platform designed to simplify scholarship discovery, application, verification, and monitoring for tribal students. The platform brings students, administrators, and scholarship information together in one accessible system.



\## 🎯 Problem



Tribal students often face difficulties in:



\* Finding relevant scholarship opportunities

\* Understanding eligibility requirements

\* Tracking application status

\* Accessing information from multiple sources

\* Completing the scholarship process efficiently



Administrators also face challenges in monitoring applications, verifying beneficiaries, and maintaining organized scholarship records.



\## 💡 Solution



VANVIDHYA provides a centralized platform that enables:



\* 📚 Scholarship discovery

\* 📝 Digital scholarship application support

\* 🔎 Eligibility-based scholarship information

\* 📊 Application and beneficiary monitoring

\* 🛡️ Administrator verification

\* 📈 Scholarship analytics and insights

\* 👨‍🎓 Student-focused access to opportunities
